# CONSOLIDATION — from LOD to a buildable machine

**Working artifact, rev 1 (2026-08-20). REGENERATED, not appended.**

## 0. Why this exists, and the rule it runs under

> *"Iteration will not save us here, because it inherits past errors at every
> step, expanding the solution space faster than we narrow it. We have to
> consolidate."*
>
> *"There is no 'verified instrument' other than established physics. We have to
> start from LOD, and work toward a buildable machine using what we have in
> METHODOLOGY.md."*

🔑 **THERE IS NO VERIFIED INSTRUMENT.** A solver is never "verified" — it agrees
or disagrees with physics on particular cases. **Established physics is the only
anchor.** The simulator is an instrument we point at the questions physics cannot
reach directly, and `METHODOLOGY.md` is the list of ways it lies.

🔴 **The register expanded because every question was generated by the previous
result's uncertainty** — R99→R101→R103→R105→R106→R107→R109→R110→R111→R112→R113.
An inward-facing loop with no external anchor can only grow. This document faces
outward: it starts at the **detection limit**, which is a physical requirement,
and works down to numbers that can be cut, ordered and assembled.

**RULE FOR THIS PHASE: no new register items.** Surprises go on a list at §5.
They do not spawn runs.

## 1. The chain, from the terminal objective down

```
        LOD  ≈  3 · σ_background / sensitivity
```

| LOD term | set by | what the CAVITY controls |
|---|---|---|
| **sensitivity** | excitation × collection × introduction | **delivered power** → electron density/temperature; **viewport étendue** → collection |
| **σ_background** | continuum + N₂ bands + stray light | **wall reflectance in the optical path** → the light trap |
| **noise / RSD** | plasma flicker | **deposition uniformity**; **frequency stability** |
| **linearity** | self-absorption on Ca/Mg/Na/K | **retroreflection**; plasma chord length |

🔑 **Everything the EM work has ever produced enters LOD through exactly two
doors: delivered power, and the optical path.** Anything that cannot be traced to
one of those is not a design input, whatever else it is.

## 4. 🔴 What actually blocks the build — and none of it is EM

| | | blocks |
|---|---|---|
| **R92** | spectrometer f-number | viewport diameter, trap diameter, **and** the axial extent of the diamond-lapped zone — the most expensive finish in the build |
| **R87** | deposition-uniformity spec | whether C1′ = 0.46 passes. Without it, uniformity cannot be scored at all |
| **R94** | cavity temperature rise | the hot-start offset budget against −57.2 kHz/K |
| **R41** | coolant-loss interlock | AlF₃ passivation sublimes above 1276 °C; RF must cut within one thermal time constant |

🔑 **All four are external, non-EM, and three of them are single numbers someone
else already knows.** The EM design has been complete for LOD purposes since R99;
everything since has been methodology archaeology — necessary to know how much of
the record to trust, but it produced no new design input.

## 5. Surprises parked (NOT register items)

- The 9.2–11.5% Q penalty of a sapphire filter is real and smooth, but the
  mechanism is unestablished: dielectric is only **2.3%** of the loss budget, so
  it must be wall-loss redistribution. The filter has **no Energy index**, so its
  stored-energy fraction has never been measured.
- **R37**: eigenmode vs driven disagree 3.7× on ε sensitivity. Unexplained.
- `--viewport 0` could not disable the viewport (falsy-zero guard) between the
  R98 default flip and R113. Only R112 was affected.
- Deliberately lossy walls nearly double cold coupling (η 11.6% → 20.4% at
  σ = 1e7) at ~1% of lit power. Never evaluated as a design option.

## 6. What a closed-form benchmark can and cannot settle

`rig_r112.py` compares an empty right cylinder to its exact spectrum. It is worth
running **once**, but note what it is for: not to "verify the instrument", but to
put a number on the disagreement between this solver and mathematics on the one
case where mathematics is complete.

⚠️ **Three attempts at it failed on MY geometry, not on the solver** — a viewport
left on, a flag that could not turn it off, and two volumes left without a
material. Each time the disagreement looked like a solver fault. **That is the
semantic-closure failure running in reverse, and it is the strongest argument in
this document for anchoring on physics rather than on any instrument.**
